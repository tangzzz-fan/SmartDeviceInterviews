# 英文沟通素材

> 用法：自我介绍背熟；项目介绍按框架练 3 遍以上；daily sync 脚本直接套用。术语保持前后一致（notify/characteristic/bonding 等）。

## 一、60 秒英文自我介绍

**模板**：

Hi, I'm 【Name】, an iOS engineer with 【N】 years of experience, most recently focused on 【BLE wearables / IoT / performance】.

I've shipped 【X apps / the companion app for a smart wearable】 from 0 to 1, owning architecture, BLE communication, and release quality. On the BLE side, I've designed connection and data-sync flows, OTA upgrades, and recovery mechanisms in close collaboration with firmware engineers. On the engineering side, I built CI/CD, automated testing, and stability monitoring that kept crash-free sessions above 99.7%.

I'm highly AI-native: I use AI across coding, test generation, debugging, and code review, and I'm comfortable working in English with distributed teams.

I'm excited about Nirva because it combines my strengths — iOS engineering, hardware collaboration, and 0-to-1 ownership — around a product I genuinely believe in.

## 二、英文项目介绍框架（5–10 分钟）

结构：What it is → My role → Architecture → Hardest problem → Result → What I'd do differently。

**示例（BLE 数据同步项目）**：

*This app pairs with a wearable device over BLE. My role was the iOS lead: I owned the architecture, the BLE layer, and the data-sync pipeline.*

*Architecture-wise, the app acts as a gateway. The device pushes sensor data over BLE; the app validates each packet, persists it locally first, then syncs to the cloud in the background. For BLE, we negotiate MTU and connection parameters per use case — tight interval during sync, relaxed in idle to save power. We added sequence numbers and CRC so the app can resume from an offset if the connection drops.*

*The hardest problem was reliability: the device kept disconnecting in the EVT phase. I isolated the issue using a sniffer and direct GATT tests, aligned the timeline with firmware logs, and we found the firmware buffer was the bottleneck. After the fix, disconnects went to nearly zero.*

*As a result, OTA success rate reached 99.x%, and the same protocol is reused in production testing. If I did it again, I'd add contract tests between app and firmware earlier.*

## 三、Daily Sync 模板

**汇报（3 句）**：

*Yesterday I finished 【X】. Today I'm working on 【Y】; it's about 【60%】 done. I need 【input from firmware / a device / a decision on Z】 — can we align on it?*

**被卡住时**：

*I'm blocked on 【X】 because 【reason】. I've tried 【A】 and 【B】. My plan is 【C】, and I need 【D】 to unblock. Does that work?*

**表示异议**：

*I see the tradeoff differently — from the data I have, 【your point】. Could we validate 【my suggestion】 with a quick prototype before committing?*

## 四、技术解释模板（把复杂讲简单）

结构：One-line answer → Why it matters → Concrete example。

示例：

*"Why does the connection keep dropping?"*

*BLE connections have a supervision timeout — if the device and phone don't exchange packets within that window, the link is considered dead. In our case the device was busy handling large writes, so it missed the window. We solved it by tuning the connection parameters and adding app-side retry with backoff.*

## 五、反问面试官问题清单（准备 3–5 个）

* What does the first 6 months look like for this role — what would "success" mean?
* How does the iOS team work with the firmware team in Shenzhen and the product team in Silicon Valley? What's the daily rhythm?
* Where is the product in the hardware cycle — EVT, DVT, or beyond?
* What's the biggest technical challenge you expect the iOS engineer to own first?
* How does the team currently use AI in the development workflow?

## 六、高频英文题速答要点

| 问题 | 答法要点 |
| --- | --- |
| Tell me about yourself | 用第一部分的 60 秒模板 |
| Why do you want to join us? | 方向认同（AI 可穿戴）+ 0→1 ownership + 软硬一体挑战 |
| Describe a difficult technical problem | 用 STAR：背景→判断→动作→数字结果 |
| How do you handle disagreement with firmware engineers? | 数据 + 原型验证，先对齐目标再谈方案 |
| How do you use AI in your work? | 3 个具体案例 + 你负责判断力 |
| What are your weaknesses? | 真实弱点 + 已建立的补偿机制，不装完美 |
