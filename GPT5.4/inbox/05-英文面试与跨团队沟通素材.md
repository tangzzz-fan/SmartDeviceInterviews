# 英文面试与跨团队沟通素材

## 使用目标

这份材料的目标不是让你说得像演讲，而是让你在英文面试和日常跨团队协作里：

- 说得清楚
- 结构稳定
- 遇到紧张也不容易散

## 技术解释模板

### 解释 BLE / 连接管理

“From my perspective, BLE communication is not just about calling the Bluetooth APIs. The real challenge is managing the connection lifecycle reliably, including discovery, connection, service setup, state synchronization, and recovery from failures such as disconnects or timeouts. In production, I would treat it as a stateful system rather than a one-time request-response flow.”

### 解释稳定性治理

“When I work on stability, I try to avoid isolated bug fixing. I prefer to build a feedback loop: define key metrics, improve observability, identify the highest-frequency failures, and validate improvements with data. That approach is usually more scalable than reacting to issues one by one.”

### 解释 AI-native 工作流

“I use AI as a force multiplier across the development workflow, not just as a coding assistant. For example, I use it to break down implementation options, draft test cases, summarize logs, and improve technical documentation. At the same time, I’m careful not to delegate critical judgment, especially around protocol design, performance-sensitive code, and release decisions.”

## 风险同步模板

### 表达 blocker

“Here’s the current blocker: the app is sending the command successfully, but we still don’t have evidence that the device is acknowledging it correctly. At this point, I think the next step is to align on logs from both sides and verify whether the issue is in the BLE transport layer, the protocol handling, or the firmware state machine.”

### 表达不确定性

“I don’t want to jump to a conclusion yet. Based on the current evidence, I have two working hypotheses, and I’d like to validate them with logs before we decide on the fix.”

### 表达下一步

“My proposed next step is to narrow the problem down layer by layer, starting from the app logs, then the BLE message flow, and then the firmware-side handling. That should help us avoid guessing and shorten the debug cycle.”

## 会议里常用句子

- “Let me clarify the problem statement first.”
- “Here’s my current understanding.”
- “I think there are two separate issues here.”
- “The key question is whether the state mismatch starts on the app side or the device side.”
- “Can we align on the expected behavior before discussing the fix?”
- “What evidence do we already have, and what is still missing?”
- “I’d prefer to validate this with logs rather than rely on assumptions.”
- “The short-term fix and the long-term fix may be different.”

## 面试官常见英文问题

### Why are you interested in this role?

回答方向：

- iOS + hardware integration 的复合难题
- ownership 强
- 0 到 1 的环境
- 对产品交付完整链路感兴趣

Sample answer:

“I’m interested in this role because it goes far beyond building a regular mobile app. It sits at the intersection of iOS engineering, device communication, system reliability, and product delivery, which is exactly the kind of work I enjoy. I’m especially drawn to roles where I can take strong ownership, work closely with cross-functional teams, and help build both the product and the engineering foundation from an early stage.”

### Tell me about a challenging technical problem.

回答方向：

- 选一个联调或稳定性问题
- 强调你如何分层定位
- 强调结果和复盘

Sample answer:

“One challenging problem I worked on involved an intermittent state mismatch between the app and an external system. The difficult part was that the issue crossed multiple layers, so it wasn’t obvious whether the root cause was on the client side, the communication layer, or the downstream component. I approached it by breaking the flow into observable stages, aligning logs across the different parts of the system, and narrowing the issue down with evidence instead of assumptions. We eventually identified the root cause, fixed it, and improved our observability so similar issues could be detected much faster in the future.”

### How do you work with cross-functional teams?

回答方向：

- 结构化同步
- 明确接口边界
- 基于证据而不是观点推进

Sample answer:

“I try to make cross-functional collaboration as structured as possible. I prefer to align early on expected behavior, interface boundaries, and what evidence we need when something goes wrong. When issues appear, I focus on shared facts such as logs, timestamps, and reproducible cases, because that helps the team avoid unnecessary debate and move more quickly toward a solution.”

### How do you use AI in your work?

回答方向：

- 举 2 到 3 个真实工作流
- 讲收益，也讲边界

Sample answer:

“I use AI as a productivity layer across the development lifecycle. For example, I use it to compare implementation options, draft test cases, summarize logs, and improve technical documentation or English communication. At the same time, I don’t rely on it blindly for critical decisions. For protocol design, debugging conclusions, performance-sensitive code, and release readiness, I always validate the output with direct evidence and engineering judgment.”

## 练习建议

1. 所有英文答案先练“短版”，每题 30 到 60 秒。
2. 再练“长版”，每题 90 秒。
3. 先求稳定清楚，再求高级表达。
4. 录音回听，重点改停顿、重复和句子过长的问题。
